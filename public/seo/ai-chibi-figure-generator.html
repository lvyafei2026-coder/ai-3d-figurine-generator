What defines a chibi figurine?

The proportions do most of the work: a larger head, smaller body, simplified facial details and a friendly expression. A smooth vinyl or plastic-like surface and a clean product-photo setup can make the character look like a small collectible toy rather than a flat cartoon.

Create a chibi figure from your picture

Upload a clear photo, choose Chibi in the style selector and start generation. The tool uses its built-in chibi style prompt, so you do not need to describe proportions or materials yourself. When the image is ready, download it as a PNG.

Tips for a recognizable chibi character

A front-facing photo with visible hair, face and outfit is a useful starting point. Avoid heavy filters and strong shadows. If you want the chibi version to keep a particular hairstyle or clothing color, make sure those details are visible in the original picture.

Image, not a 3D file: Toolara creates a downloadable PNG with a 3D figurine appearance. It does not currently export STL or OBJ models for 3D printing.

Frequently asked questions

Can I make a chibi figure from a selfie?

Yes. A clear selfie or portrait can be used as the reference photo. A photo that shows more of the outfit can help preserve clothing details.

Is the result an actual 3D model?

No. The generator creates a 2D PNG image rendered to look like a 3D chibi figurine. It does not export a printable 3D mesh.

Can I try different figure styles?

Yes. Return to the main generator to try other presets such as Action figure, Doll, Realistic, Anime, Plush and Clay.
