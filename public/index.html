Frequently asked questions

Is this tool free?

Yes. It runs on Cloudflare Workers AI with a free daily quota. Normal use won't hit limits.

Is this an AI action figure generator?

Yes. The Action figure preset renders you as a collectible figure in blister packaging, and the Doll preset renders a soft-vinyl doll. Both work from a photo — you don't have to write a prompt.

Is the result a real 3D model?

No. You get a high-resolution image rendered in a 3D figurine style — not an .obj or .stl file you can print. Think of it as a preview of the figure, made for sharing or mockups.

Is my photo stored?

No. Your photo is processed in memory and discarded immediately after the result is generated. Nothing is stored.

What kind of photo works best?

Front-facing portraits with clear lighting and a plain background work best. For Action figure or Doll, a full-body or half-body shot gives a better result than a tight face crop. Avoid blurry, low-light, or heavily filtered images.

Why does generation sometimes fail?

The AI model has a content filter that occasionally misidentifies harmless images. If generation fails, try a different photo with a simpler background.

Can I use the result commercially?

Yes. The tool doesn't add watermarks. You own the result, but make sure you have the rights to the original photo.

Note: AI-generated images are for entertainment. Do not use them for official identification or commercial licensing without review.
