What does this tool do?

This tool uses AI to turn your photo into a 3D figurine style image. Pick a style — chibi, realistic, pixel, or cyberpunk — and see how you'd look as a collectible figure. It's a fun way to visualize yourself in a new format.

Frequently asked questions

Is this tool free?

Yes. It runs on Cloudflare Workers AI with a free daily quota. Normal use won't hit limits.

Is my photo stored?

No. Your photo is processed in memory and discarded immediately after the result is generated. Nothing is stored.

What kind of photo works best?

Front-facing portraits with clear lighting and a plain background work best. Avoid blurry, low-light, or heavily filtered images.

Why does generation sometimes fail?

The AI model has a content filter that occasionally misidentifies harmless images. If generation fails, try a different photo with a simpler background.

Can I use the result commercially?

Yes. The tool doesn't add watermarks. You own the result, but make sure you have the rights to the original photo.

Note: AI-generated images are for entertainment. Do not use them for official identification or commercial licensing without review.
